# 🏦 Salsabilah City RTGS & AI Engine

An enterprise-grade, autonomous RTGS (Real-Time Gross Settlement) banking and Gemini AI integration backend built for **Salsabilah Empires** and **S.R. Electronics Park** to automate B2B transactions and supply chain settlements.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **AI-First Invoice Processing:** Uses **Google Gemini API** to extract payment details seamlessly.
* **Automated RTGS Integration:** Connects with **City Bank PLC** and Bangladesh Bank RTGS switch architecture.
* **Built-in Financial Logic:** Automatically calculates standard RTGS charges (BDT 100) and 15% VAT.
* **Risk Guardrails:** Validates routing numbers and account details against official records.
